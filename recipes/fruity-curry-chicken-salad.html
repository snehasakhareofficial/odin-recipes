Fruity Curry Chicken Salad

Description

This creamy curry chicken salad gets lots of flavor from curry powder and texture from green grapes, apples, and celery for a delicious fruity twist on the classic chicken salad. Tastes great on a croissant, on a bed of lettuce, or in a sandwich.

Ingredients

  • 4 cooked chicken breasts, diced
  • 1 rib celery, diced
  • 4 green onions, chopped
  • 1 Golden Delicious apple - peeled, cored and diced
  • ⅓ cup golden raisins
  • ⅓ cup seedless green grapes, halved
  • ½ cup chopped toasted pecans
  • ⅛ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon curry powder or more to taste
  • ¾ cup light or nonfat mayonnaise

Steps

  1. Combine chicken, celery, onion, apple, raisins, grapes, pecans, pepper, curry powder, and mayonnaise in a bowl; mix well to combine.
